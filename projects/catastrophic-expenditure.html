Recovery and Recurrence after Catastrophic Health Expenditure

This project studies what happens after a household first experiences catastrophic health expenditure: how quickly it recovers and whether it later experiences another episode.

DataKorea Health Panel
DesignDiscrete-time survival
Period2011–2018

Data & Research Question

Households are followed after their first observed episode of out-of-pocket medical spending equal to at least 10% of annual household income.

Empirical Approach

Complementary log-log discrete-time survival models estimate recovery and recurrence patterns, with particular attention to socioeconomic gradients.

Current Status

Preliminary findings indicate large income-related differences in both recovery and recurrence. The manuscript is being revised for clearer interpretation of these dynamics.

This page presents an ongoing research project. Results and descriptions may change as the analysis develops.
