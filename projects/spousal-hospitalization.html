Working paper

Spousal Hospitalization and Private Insurance Decisions

This project asks whether the hospitalization of a spouse changes an older adult’s private health insurance decisions.

[data]
DataKLoSA 2006–2024
DesignStacked difference-in-differences
OutcomePrivate insurance

Data & Research Question

The Korean Longitudinal Study of Aging provides repeated observations on hospitalization, insurance coverage, household characteristics, and socioeconomic status.

Empirical Approach

A stacked difference-in-differences and event-study design compares respondents around the timing of a spouse’s first hospitalization with appropriate not-yet-treated or never-treated controls.

Current Status

Preliminary results suggest little new take-up among initially uninsured respondents but increased discontinuation among those who were insured before the event.

This page presents an ongoing research project. Results and descriptions may change as the analysis develops.
