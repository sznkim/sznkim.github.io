Financial Burden and Health Investment Decisions

This project examines whether changes in the financial burden of medical expenditure affect preventive healthcare use and health-related behaviors among U.S. households.

DataMEPS 1996–2023
DesignLongitudinal panel analysis
StatusAPHA 2026

Data & Research Question

The analysis uses longitudinal Medical Expenditure Panel Survey data and focuses on within-person variation in medical expenditure burden. Outcomes include preventive services, smoking, and physical activity.

Empirical Approach

The empirical framework uses panel logistic models with year fixed effects and correlated random effects. The project distinguishes persistent cross-sectional differences from changes occurring within the same person over time.

Current Status

The abstract was accepted for presentation in the APHA Medical Care Section Student Paper Award session. The full manuscript is in preparation.
