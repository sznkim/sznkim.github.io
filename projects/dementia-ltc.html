Ongoing project

Dementia, Long-Term Care, and Administrative Data

This project uses linked Korean health insurance and social security administrative data to study dementia-related care pathways and long-term care use.

[NHIS] [SNU AI Institute]
DataLinked Korean administrative records
FocusDementia and long-term care
MethodsPolicy evaluation · ML

Data & Research Question

The data combine diagnosis and healthcare utilization records with long-term care assessment scores, eligibility grades, validity periods, and selected welfare information.

Empirical Approach

The project is being developed in two directions: interpretable prediction of adverse outcomes among people with dementia, and causal evaluation of policy changes affecting access to formal long-term care.

Current Status

The current stage focuses on cohort construction, validation of dementia definitions, linkage quality, and scalable data processing.

This page presents an ongoing research project. Results and descriptions may change as the analysis develops.
