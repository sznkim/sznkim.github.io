Work in progress

Work Conditions and Unmet Healthcare Needs

A panel-data study of work conditions and unmet healthcare needs using the Korean Health Panel Survey.

Research Focus

The project examines whether changes in employment and working conditions are associated with changes in unmet healthcare needs.

Approach

The study uses longitudinal Korean Health Panel data and panel regression methods.

Current Status

The project is ongoing.
