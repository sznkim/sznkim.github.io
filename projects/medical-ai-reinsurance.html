Award-winning team project

Reinsurance Architecture for Systemic Misdiagnosis Risk in Healthcare AI

This team project developed an insurance and reinsurance architecture for systemic misdiagnosis risks generated by healthcare AI.

FormatPolicy and insurance design
ToolR Shiny
MethodsMonte Carlo simulation

Data & Research Question

The framework links primary insurance and reinsurance and evaluates seven dimensions of medical AI risk. It was designed to help translate technical model risk into an operational insurance structure.

Empirical Approach

I developed an interactive R Shiny Medical AI Reinsurance Structuring Engine with Monte Carlo simulation to examine loss distributions and alternative contract structures.

Current Status

The project was selected as an award recipient in a competition organized by Samsung Fire & Marine, POSTECH, and the SNU AI ecosystem.
