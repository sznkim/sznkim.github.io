Impact of Earthquakes on Orthopedic Injury-related Hospital Admissions

A systematic review of orthopedic injury-related hospital admissions following earthquakes of magnitude 6.5 or greater.

Research Focus

The project synthesizes evidence on hospital utilization related to orthopedic injuries after major earthquakes. The review was expanded to include 36 studies.

Approach

Systematic review methods were used to identify, screen, and synthesize studies reporting orthopedic injury-related hospital utilization after major earthquakes.

Current Status

The manuscript is under review. An earlier version received an Excellence Award in the SNU BK21 student paper competition and was presented orally at the Korean Society of Emergency Medicine annual conference.
