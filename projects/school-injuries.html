Submitted manuscript

Socioeconomic Inequalities in School Injuries

A study of socioeconomic inequalities in school injuries and the mediating role of protective behaviors among children.

Research Focus

The study examines whether socioeconomic differences in childhood injury are partly explained by differences in protective behaviors.

Approach

The empirical analysis uses mediation methods to separate direct and indirect pathways.

Current Status

The manuscript has been submitted to the Journal of Community Health.
