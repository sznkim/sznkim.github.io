Hello, I’m Seojin Kim. My work lies at the intersection of health economics and applied microeconometrics, with a focus on healthcare utilization, financial risk, insurance, and policy evaluation.

During my graduate studies, I worked in the Office of the Chief Economist for Infrastructure at the World Bank Group, where I supported cross-country economic analyses of infrastructure investment and resource allocation.
