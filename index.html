About

Health Economics · Applied Microeconometrics · Health Policy

I am an MPH candidate in Healthcare Management and Policy, with a concentration in Health Economics, at Seoul National University. My research interests include health economics, applied microeconometrics, health policy, cost-effectiveness analysis, geospatial analysis, child health, and palliative and hospice care.

My current work examines medical expenditure burden, unmet healthcare needs, parental labor supply, private insurance decisions, long-term care, and administrative health data.

MethodsPanel data, DiD, survival analysis, complex surveys, systematic reviews
ProgrammingStata, R, Python, SAS, SPSS, MATLAB, Julia, SQL
ApplicationsMedical expenditure, insurance, child health, palliative care, disasters
