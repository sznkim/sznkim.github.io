Research Interests

My research combines health economics, applied microeconometrics, and machine learning to study healthcare use, financial protection, insurance behavior, and long-term care.

Health Economics

Medical Expenditure and Financial Protection

Medical expenditure burden, preventive healthcare use, catastrophic health expenditure, CHE recovery, and CHE recurrence.

Applied Microeconometrics

Insurance and Labor Supply

Anlysis using microecometric modelings (Stacked DID, Staggered DID), Longitudinal analyses of insurance decisions, family health shocks, work conditions, and parental labor supply.

Machine Learning & Administrative Data

Dementia and Long-Term Care using Large Claim Datasets

Machine learning and policy evaluation using linked health insurance and social security administrative records in South Korea.
