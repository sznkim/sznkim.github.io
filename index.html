About

I am an MPH candidate in Health Policy and Health Economics at Seoul National University. My research examines how financial and institutional constraints shape healthcare use, insurance decisions, and health investment.

I am particularly interested in applied microeconometrics, administrative health data, causal inference, and interpretable machine learning for policy research. My recent work uses longitudinal survey and administrative data from Korea and the United States.

Previously, I worked as a research assistant in the Office of the Chief Economist for Infrastructure at the World Bank Group, supporting analyses of infrastructure stocks and the social rates of return on infrastructure investment across more than 150 countries.
