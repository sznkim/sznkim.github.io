Seojin Kim

MPH Candidate, Seoul National University Graduate School of Public Health

I am a health economics researcher studying how medical expenditure, family health shocks, and policy institutions shape healthcare use, insurance decisions, and household welfare. I combine longitudinal surveys and large-scale administrative data with applied microeconometric methods.

Previously, I worked in the World Bank Group’s Office of the Chief Economist for Infrastructure, supporting cross-country analyses of infrastructure stocks and the social rates of return on investment across more than 150 countries.
